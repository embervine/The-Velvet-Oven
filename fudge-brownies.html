Midnight Fudge Brownies

Dense chocolate squares with that paper-thin crackly top.

◷ 45 min✦ Easy
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 180 g dark chocolate
  • 140 g butter
  • 1 cup caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup plain flour
  • ¼ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp salt
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Heat oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line an 8-inch square tin.
  2. Melt butter and chocolate together; cool slightly.
  3. Beat eggs and sugar until glossy, then whisk in chocolate mixture.
  4. Fold in flour, cocoa and salt until just combined.
  5. Bake 22–28 minutes until edges are set and centre is slightly fudgy. Cool before slicing.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
