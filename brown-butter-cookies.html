Brown Butter Chocolate Cookies

Crisp edges, gooey middles, pools of dark chocolate.

◷ 35 min✦ Easy
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 170 g unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1¾ cups plain flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 170 g dark chocolate
  • ½ tsp flaky salt
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Brown butter in a saucepan until nutty and amber; cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk butter with both sugars. Add egg and vanilla.
  3. Mix in flour and baking soda; fold through chopped chocolate.
  4. Scoop onto lined trays and chill 15 minutes.
  5. Bake at 180°C / 350°F for 10–12 minutes. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
