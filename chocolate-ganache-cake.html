Velvet Chocolate Celebration Cake

Our signature two-layer cocoa cake with glossy ganache.

◷ 1 hr 15 min✦ Intermediate
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 1¾ cups flour
  • 1¾ cups sugar
  • ¾ cup cocoa powder
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup oil
  • 1 cup hot coffee
  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 200 ml heavy cream
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Heat oven to 175°C / 350°F. Grease two 8-inch tins.
  2. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa and baking powder. Whisk in eggs, milk and oil.
  3. Carefully stir in hot coffee. Batter will be thin.
  4. Bake 30–35 minutes; cool fully.
  5. Pour heated cream over chopped chocolate; stir to a glossy ganache. Fill and frost cake.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
