Strawberry Cloud Cake

Fresh strawberries, whipped vanilla cream and the softest sponge.

◷ 55 min✦ Intermediate
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cake flour
  • 1 cup caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup whipping cream
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Grease two 8-inch cake tins.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar until thick and pale. Fold in sifted flour.
  3. Warm milk and butter together. Stir in vanilla, then fold gently into batter.
  4. Divide between tins and bake for 25–30 minutes. Cool completely.
  5. Whip cream to soft peaks; slice strawberries. Layer cake, cream and berries. Chill before serving.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
