Slow Sunday Cinnamon Rolls

Warm, fluffy swirls with a generous cream-cheese glaze.

◷ 2 hr 30 min✦ Intermediate
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 3½ cups flour
  • 2¼ tsp instant yeast
  • ¾ cup warm milk
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp cinnamon
  • 3 tbsp soft butter
  • ½ cup cream cheese
  • ¾ cup icing sugar
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Mix flour, yeast, warm milk, sugar, egg and melted butter; knead until smooth.
  2. Cover and rise for 60–75 minutes, until doubled.
  3. Roll into a rectangle. Spread soft butter and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar.
  4. Roll tightly, cut into 9 pieces, and rise another 30 minutes.
  5. Bake at 180°C / 350°F for 22–27 minutes. Beat cream cheese with icing sugar and spread over warm rolls.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
