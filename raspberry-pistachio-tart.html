Raspberry Pistachio Tart

A buttery shell, silky pistachio filling and bright berries.

◷ 1 hr 20 min✦ Intermediate
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 1 ready-made 9-inch tart shell
  • ¾ cup ground pistachios
  • ½ cup softened butter
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1½ cups raspberries
  • 2 tbsp chopped pistachios
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C / 350°F.
  2. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs, then ground pistachios and flour.
  3. Spread filling into tart shell. Bake 30–35 minutes until just set.
  4. Cool completely, top with raspberries and chopped pistachios.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
