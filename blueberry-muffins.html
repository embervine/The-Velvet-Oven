Bakery-Style Blueberry Muffins

Tall, golden tops and blueberries in every bite.

◷ 40 min✦ Easy
What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1½ cups blueberries
Step by step

Let's make it

  1. Heat oven to 200°C / 400°F and line a 12-cup muffin tray.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients in one bowl and wet ingredients in another.
  3. Fold wet into dry; gently add blueberries.
  4. Divide between cups and bake 18–22 minutes until golden.

Tip: Read the whole method before you start, and allow baked goods to cool before frosting or slicing.
